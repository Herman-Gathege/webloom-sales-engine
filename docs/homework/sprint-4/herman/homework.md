# Herman — Sprint 4

## Start here

Open [workflow.html](../../../workflow.html), then read
[workflow-description.md](../../../workflow-description.md). Skim the
[full Wadi guide](../../../full-wadi-documentation.md) for status and roadmap context. You are
reviewing the Catalog as the product driver, not redesigning the application.

## Your mission

Make the Catalog feel like part of Wadi and confirm it works at every screen size.

## What to do

- Review Offers and Templates on desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Check card alignment, form spacing and navigation.
- Confirm Admin sees controls and Sales sees read-only records.
- Exercise empty, loading and error states.
- Record only concrete UI or usability issues.

## Done means

- [ ] Catalog navigation is clear.
- [ ] Mobile cards do not create horizontal sliding.
- [ ] Admin and Sales states are understandable.
- [ ] No unrelated screen has regressed.

## Bring back

Screenshots and a short issue list with reproduction steps.

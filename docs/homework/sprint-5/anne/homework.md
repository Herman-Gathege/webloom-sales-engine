# Anne — Sprint 5 Seller and Copy Review

## Your mission

Review the campaign and draft workflow as the seller. Mark and Sharon cover technical safety;
your job is whether the flow, copy and skip reasons make sense in real sales work.

## What to review

- Walk through Campaign List → Create/Preview → Campaign Detail.
- Confirm a seller can tell which leads will receive drafts and why others were skipped.
- Review the current SMS seed and identify what the approved A/B and social-only copy still
  needs.
- Confirm price wording remains truthful when offer guidance is blank.
- Review channel length, opt-out wording and whether segments match how you actually target.
- Confirm Draft versus Skip information is understandable without reading backend docs.
- Confirm the UI clearly stops before approval and sending.

## Done means

- [ ] Seller journey is clear.
- [ ] Copy gaps and exact replacement wording are recorded.
- [ ] Skip reasons are understandable.
- [ ] No code or message sending is performed.

## Bring back

Approve/revise verdict, 3–5 concrete UX findings, and approved or replacement copy.

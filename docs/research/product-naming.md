# Product Naming

## What we're naming

A short everyday name for the Webloom Sales Engine — the tool that does the repetitive ~80% of prospecting (find → research → segment → score → personalise → reach → listen → qualify → human handoff → outcome → learn) so a person can do the 20% that closes. It has to keep working as the product learns which sectors, profiles, messages and channels are worth our attention, so it must not read as "lead generation" or "SMS blasting". We want the name people say without thinking: *"Jicho found 20 leads."*

## Shortlist

*Screened for obvious collisions only — npm, PyPI, Wikipedia, GitHub, the Kenyan App Store, and Kenyan search results. This is not trademark clearance, and a human pass is still needed.*

| Name | Meaning/idea | Why it fits | Main concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jicho** | Swahili: eye (*macho* = eyes) | The "extra eyes on the market" story, and it maps to handoff — automation looks, a person looks closer | The local association is *Jicho Pevu*, a Kenyan investigative TV strand (we could not verify this from here — check it locally); singular is *jicho*, plural *macho* |
| **Fursa** | Swahili: opportunity — "a chance for advancement, progress or profit" | Names the product's output, not its method, so it survives past lead-gen; short and natural to say | A common noun, so un-ownable in search; "Fursa – Buy & Sell Anything" is already in the Kenyan App Store |
| **Lenga** | Swahili: to aim (at), to strive for | Targeting, and the root of *lengo* — so the product's own vocabulary can grow later (*malengo* = objectives) | Least distinctive: a Kenyan surname, a Patagonian tree genus, and Occitan for "language" |
| **Nia** | Swahili: intention, purpose | Warm, two syllables, and it names what the engine acts on — intent, not broadcast | Un-ownable: an npm library, a PyPI package, "Nia Payments" in the Kenyan App Store, dozens of NIA acronyms |
| **Kibali** | Swahili: approval, consent (plural *vibali*) | Names our loudest rule — a human approves every send — and it is the cleanest namespace we found (56 GitHub matches) | Three syllables; Kibali Awards and Kibali Africa Tours are in Nairobi; Kibali is also a DRC gold mine |
| **Tambua** | Swahili: to recognise, perceive, realise | Describes reading intent before it is spoken — the qualification promise | A Kenyan organisation appears to sit on tambua.co.ke |
| **Tafuta** | Swahili: to search | The find step, instantly understood by any Swahili speaker | Reads like a command; generic verb; tafuta.co.ke is registered |
| **Chuja** | Swahili: to filter, sieve, separate | The single best match to the first job — filtering noise until what is left is opportunity | Three strikes: a PyPI package, a vulgar word in Polish and Slovak, and a Kenyan campaign already called CHUJA |
| **Talo** | invented, no meaning | Strongest pure-invented option: free on npm and PyPI, nothing in the Kenyan App Store, easy to say | No story to hang on it, and in Kenya it sits one letter from **Tala**, a well-known lender |
| **Kibo** | place: Kibo is Kilimanjaro's highest peak | East African and ambitious — the summit people aim for | npm is taken; also Japan's ISS module (Kibō) |
| **Chagua** | Swahili: to choose, elect | Names the qualification step — choosing which leads deserve a human | Effectively owned by Kenyan voter education (chagua.co.ke, "Chagua Kenya 2027"); politically loaded |
| **Weft** | the crosswise thread woven through the warp | The weave metaphor lines up with *Webloom* itself | Worst namespace of the set: an AI-orchestration language (2k★) and an agent-infrastructure package use it |

Confirmed in local use and dropped: **Songa** (a Kenyan music brand — "Songa Play" is in the Kenyan App Store and `songa.co.ke` is registered), **Dira**, **Lengo**, **Vuna**, **Kua**, **Pata**, **Kuna**.

Two honest gaps. We could not independently confirm the owner behind every dropped name, so read that list as "already in use locally", not a verified register. And nothing here captures Nairobi slang or street connotations — say each shortlisted name out loud in a sentence at the meeting before deciding.

The conceptual English family came up empty: all fourteen candidates tested (sift, sieve, riffle, trawl, winnow, baton, relay, bearing, vane, azimuth, keel, weft, kestrel, spyglass) are already registered on **both** npm and PyPI, and most are established products. The English-metaphor direction is mined out.

## Top 5

1. **Jicho** — the cleanest name checked: no Kenyan app, no Wikipedia entity, ~60 harmless GitHub matches. The one local association is Jicho Pevu, KTN's investigative strand, which arguably *helps* the "extra eyes" story — but decide whether it sounds like a news brand.
2. **Fursa** — the best meaning fit: it names what the tool produces (opportunity) rather than how it works, so it will not date when the product learns new tricks. It is free on npm and PyPI; accept that as a common noun you will never own it in search.
3. **Lenga** — "to aim" is short, active, free on both npm and PyPI, and unused by any Kenyan business. Being the least distinctive of the five is its only real flaw.
4. **Nia** — the nicest word to say and the closest to the pitch; choose it with your eyes open that npm, PyPI, an App Store payments app and dozens of NIA acronyms already use it, so it always needs "Webloom" beside it.
5. **Kibali** — the only candidate that names our non-negotiable (a human approves every send), and the cleanest namespace by GitHub count. Three syllables is the price.
